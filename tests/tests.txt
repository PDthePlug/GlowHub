# Tests

## Test 1 - Discover a published business and open its profile [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: Discover landing, GET /api/discover, public storefront discovery projection, search by service/location/date, storefront navigation
Description: Verifies Glow Hub behaves as a customer discovery destination and only surfaces a real published business.

Setup:
1. Sign in as a business owner and create a business named "Discovery Studio" in Johannesburg with a service named "Silk Press" priced at R450, leave online booking enabled, and keep the selected search date open in Working hours

Steps:
1. Sign out or return to the Glow Hub home page
2. Search for "Silk Press" in Johannesburg on the open date
3. Open the Discovery Studio result

Expected: Discovery Studio appears as a real search result with its service and price, and opening it shows the customer-facing business profile rather than the old SaaS marketing landing page.

## Test 2 - Profile-first storefront leads directly from services to booking
Viewport: mobile
Covers: storefront profile layout, services list, sticky booking action, service preset, public booking journey
Description: Verifies the redesigned storefront keeps the customer task focused on choosing a service and real available time.

Setup:
1. Sign in as a business owner and create a business with one active service and at least one open working day, then open its public website

Steps:
1. Review the business summary, photo area and Services section on mobile
2. Tap Book for the active service
3. Choose an available date and time and submit a valid booking request

Expected: The storefront stays inside the mobile viewport, the selected service carries into booking, only real available times are shown, and the request is received successfully.

## Test 3 - Discovery gives a useful empty state instead of invented businesses
Viewport: desktop
Covers: discovery search guardrail, no-result state, no fake marketplace data
Description: Verifies a search with no real published match does not fabricate listings, ratings or popularity.

Steps:
1. Open the Glow Hub discovery home
2. Search for "Nonexistent Quantum Treatment 999" in "Nowhere 999"
3. Review the results area

Expected: A clear no-matching-businesses state is shown with an option to show all businesses, and no fake venue card, rating or booking count appears.

## Test 4 - Real reviews, photos and opening hours appear only when the business has them
Viewport: desktop
Covers: storefront profile evidence, real review rendering, portfolio photos, business-hours presentation, WhatsApp fallback
Description: Verifies the profile-style storefront uses genuine business evidence rather than decorative claims.

Setup:
1. Sign in as an owner, add one real portfolio photo and one published review to a business, and save working hours

Steps:
1. Open the public business profile
2. Scroll through Photos, Reviews and About
3. Review the opening-hours block and WhatsApp action

Expected: The uploaded work and published review appear, the saved weekly hours are shown in About, and WhatsApp remains available without any invented review or team data.

## Test 5 - Business owner flow remains separate from customer discovery
Viewport: mobile
Covers: owner sign-in regression, Today/Bookings/Clients/Growth/My Business navigation, Discover-to-business boundary, Meta/settings regression
Description: Verifies the new customer-first home does not replace or expose the private business operating experience.

Steps:
1. From Discover, choose Business sign in and sign in as an existing owner
2. Move through Today, Bookings, Clients, Growth and My Business
3. Open Settings and review the Meta connection cards

Expected: The owner enters the private business app normally, daily navigation remains usable, and Meta connections still use Connect/Not connected states without exposing tokens or secrets.
