# Tests

## Test 1 - Discover a published business and open its profile [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: Discover landing, public discovery search, published storefront projection, service/location/date filtering, storefront navigation
Description: Verifies Glow Hub behaves as a customer discovery destination and only surfaces a real published business.

Setup:
1. Sign in as a business owner and create a business named "Discovery Studio" in Johannesburg with a service named "Silk Press" priced at R450, leave online booking enabled, and keep the selected search date open in Working hours

Steps:
1. Sign out or return to the Glow Hub home page
2. Search for "Silk Press" in Johannesburg on the open date
3. Open the Discovery Studio result

Expected: Discovery Studio appears with its real service and price, and opening it shows its customer storefront rather than the private business app.

## Test 2 - Website design changes the real storefront architecture
Viewport: desktop
Covers: storefront customization persistence, live phone preview, Clean white template, Warm & soft template, Dark & bold template, accent inheritance, public storefront rendering
Description: Verifies Customize My Website selects genuinely different website compositions rather than recolouring one shared page.

Setup:
1. Sign in as an owner with a business that has hero photos, at least three portfolio images, one service and one published review

Steps:
1. Open My Business → Website, switch among Clean white, Warm & soft and Dark & bold, and confirm the phone preview changes composition for each design
2. Choose Clean white, save, and open the public storefront; then repeat for Warm & soft
3. Choose Dark & bold, save, and reopen the public storefront

Expected: Clean white uses a restrained profile header and a maximum three-photo collage, Warm & soft uses a split editorial hero with rounded imagery/card treatment, and Dark & bold uses a cinematic high-contrast hero, heavy type and harder gallery/service treatment. The same services, portfolio evidence and booking data remain intact in all three.

## Test 3 - Storefront leads from a service to a real available booking
Viewport: mobile
Covers: responsive storefront template, service preset, premium booking journey, working-hours availability, occupied-slot protection, booking request
Description: Verifies each public website keeps the customer task clear and uses the hardened availability rules.

Setup:
1. Sign in as an owner, keep one active service and one future working day open, then publish the storefront

Steps:
1. Open the public storefront on mobile and tap the service card, review Photos & details, then tap Book this service
2. Confirm the service, price and duration remain visible on the booking page
3. Choose the open date and one displayed available time
4. Enter a valid customer name and phone number and send the booking request

Expected: The chosen service carries into booking, the page stays inside the mobile viewport, only valid available times are offered, and the success screen says the request was sent to the business for confirmation.

## Test 4 - Discovery and storefronts never invent marketplace evidence
Viewport: desktop
Covers: discovery empty state, real review/photo rendering, opening hours, no fake ratings or popularity, WhatsApp fallback
Description: Verifies Glow Hub only displays customer evidence that a business actually supplied.

Setup:
1. Publish one business with a real portfolio photo and review, and leave another published business without reviews

Steps:
1. Search for a phrase/location that has no matching published business and inspect the empty state
2. Open the business with real evidence and inspect Portfolio, Reviews and About
3. Open the business without reviews and inspect its summary

Expected: No-match search shows a simple empty state rather than fabricated listings; uploaded work/reviews/hours appear where supplied; the business without reviews is shown as new or without a rating rather than receiving an invented score.

## Test 5 - Customer discovery stays separate from the owner operating app
Viewport: mobile
Covers: business sign-in boundary, Today/Bookings/Clients/Growth/My Business regression, storefront editor access, Meta connection security
Description: Verifies the marketplace direction does not weaken the private owner product or secure social-publishing boundary.

Steps:
1. From Discover, choose Business sign in and authenticate as an existing owner
2. Move through Today, Bookings, Clients, Growth and My Business, then open Website
3. Open Settings and review Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp Business connection cards

Expected: The owner enters the private app normally, Website offers the three full website designs, daily navigation remains usable, and Meta cards never expose access tokens, App Secrets or manual Page IDs.

## Test 6 - Full customer journey uses customer language and a consistent waitlist
Viewport: mobile
Covers: Discover language, storefront navigation, service gallery, booking, no-slot waitlist, confirmation, route-level loading
Description: Verifies public pages help the customer decide and act without exposing Glow Hub implementation language.

Setup:
1. Publish a business with portfolio work, one service, and a future date with no remaining availability

Steps:
1. Start on Discover and open the business, then Services → service Photos & details
2. Open booking, choose the fully booked date, and follow Join the waitlist
3. Submit the waitlist form and return to the business
4. Repeat booking on a date with availability and submit a booking request

Expected: The journey stays visually consistent from Discover through waitlist/booking, no page talks about a "published storefront", "saved availability", provider setup or internal platform mechanics, and both waitlist and booking confirmations tell the customer what happens next in plain language.

## Test 7 - Storefront theme never resets on public subpages
Viewport: mobile and desktop
Covers: Dark & bold service cards, services directory, service detail, portfolio, reviews, about, booking, waitlist, owner live preview placement
Description: Verifies the selected website design is a persistent storefront identity rather than a homepage-only skin.

Setup:
1. Publish a business using Dark & bold with at least one service, portfolio work and a future date with no remaining availability

Steps:
1. Open the Dark & bold storefront and confirm services are presented as deliberate dark cards with a visible service-detail action
2. Visit Services, one service detail page, Portfolio, Reviews and About
3. Open booking and then Join the waitlist from a full date
4. Sign in as the owner and open My Business → Website; switch among all three themes

Expected: Every customer-facing page keeps the selected business theme, Dark & bold never falls back to Clean white, booking/waitlist remain dark, the mobile booking summary and Send booking request action appear after the customer details, and the owner sees the live preview before the theme controls with Open website beside the preview.
