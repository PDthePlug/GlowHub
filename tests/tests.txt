# Tests

## Test 1 - Discover a published business and open its profile [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: Discover landing, public discovery search, published storefront projection, service/location/date filtering, storefront navigation
Description: Verifies Glow Hub behaves as a customer discovery destination and only surfaces a real published business.

Setup:
1. Sign in as a business owner and create a business named "Discovery Studio" in Johannesburg with a service named "Silk Press" priced at R450, leave online booking enabled, and keep the selected search date open in Working hours

Steps:
1. Sign out or return to the Glow Hub home page
2. Search for "Silk Press" in Johannesburg on the open date
3. Open the Discovery Studio result

Expected: Discovery Studio appears with its real service and price, and opening it shows its customer storefront rather than the private business app.

## Test 2 - Website look changes the real storefront architecture
Viewport: desktop
Covers: storefront customization persistence, Clean white template, Warm & soft template, Dark & bold template, accent inheritance, public storefront rendering
Description: Verifies Customize My Website selects genuinely different website compositions rather than recolouring one shared page.

Setup:
1. Sign in as an owner with a business that has a cover photo, at least three portfolio images, one service and one published review

Steps:
1. Open My Business → Website, choose Clean white, save, and open the public storefront
2. Return to Website, choose Warm & soft, save, and reopen the public storefront
3. Return to Website, choose Dark & bold, save, and reopen the public storefront

Expected: Clean white uses a restrained profile header and clean collage, Warm & soft uses a split editorial hero with rounded imagery/card treatment, and Dark & bold uses a cinematic high-contrast hero, heavy type and harder gallery/service treatment. The same booking/service data remains intact in all three.

## Test 3 - Profile storefront leads directly from a service to real booking
Viewport: mobile
Covers: responsive storefront template, service preset, sticky booking action, working-hours availability, occupied-slot protection, booking request
Description: Verifies each public website keeps the customer task clear and uses the hardened booking engine.

Setup:
1. Sign in as an owner, keep one active service and one future working day open, then publish the storefront

Steps:
1. Open the public storefront on mobile and tap Book for the active service
2. Choose the open date and one displayed available time
3. Submit a valid customer name and phone number

Expected: The chosen service carries into booking, the page stays inside the mobile viewport, only valid available times are offered, and the booking request is recorded successfully.

## Test 4 - Discovery and storefronts never invent marketplace evidence
Viewport: desktop
Covers: discovery empty state, real review/photo rendering, opening hours, no fake ratings or popularity, WhatsApp fallback
Description: Verifies Glow Hub only displays customer evidence that a business actually supplied.

Setup:
1. Publish one business with a real portfolio photo and review, and leave another published business without reviews

Steps:
1. Search for a phrase/location that has no matching published business and inspect the empty state
2. Open the business with real evidence and inspect Photos, Reviews and About
3. Open the business without reviews and inspect its summary

Expected: No-match search shows an empty state rather than fabricated listings; uploaded work/reviews/hours appear where supplied; the business without reviews is shown as new or without a rating rather than receiving an invented score.

## Test 5 - Customer discovery stays separate from the owner operating app
Viewport: mobile
Covers: business sign-in boundary, Today/Bookings/Clients/Growth/My Business regression, storefront editor access, Meta connection security
Description: Verifies the marketplace direction does not weaken the private owner product or secure social-publishing boundary.

Steps:
1. From Discover, choose Business sign in and authenticate as an existing owner
2. Move through Today, Bookings, Clients, Growth and My Business, then open Website
3. Open Settings and review Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp Business connection cards

Expected: The owner enters the private app normally, Website still offers the three storefront designs, daily navigation remains usable, and Meta cards never expose access tokens, App Secrets or manual Page IDs.
